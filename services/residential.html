TL;DR

Residential mosquito control means removing what's actually causing the problem — standing water and shaded resting sites — then applying barrier spray on a schedule that holds through the season. Products are EPA/PMRA-registered and safe for kids and pets once dry (about 30 minutes), and every seasonal program includes a 21-day return-visit guarantee.

Built for Homeowners, Not Adapted From One

Most residential mosquito problems come down to the same two things: standing water your property is quietly breeding in, and shaded, humid vegetation where adult mosquitoes rest during the day. A one-time spray knocks down what's active right now, but it doesn't address either root cause — which is why most homeowners end up on a seasonal program instead of a single visit.

Mosquito Man Plus is part of the Bugman Plus family, based in Oshawa. We're not a national franchise dispatching whoever's available — it's a local team that knows Durham Region and GTA properties, tailoring the treatment plan to your yard on the first visit rather than running the same script everywhere.

What's Included in Residential Service

  • Residential Barrier Treatment: the core visit. Perimeter treatment along vegetation, shade lines, fences, and resting sites so your yard stays usable through peak season.
  • Seasonal Mosquito Programs: a scheduled run of barrier treatments spaced to hatch cycles and rainfall, so coverage holds all season instead of fading a few weeks after a single spray.
  • Standing Water & Breeding Site Treatment: locating the water your property is actually breeding mosquitoes in — clogged gutters, low spots, containers, unused pools — and treating it directly at the source.
  • Larvicide Treatment: targeting mosquitoes before they can fly, in water features and low spots that can't reasonably be drained or removed outright.
  • Tick Control Add-On: bundled into a scheduled mosquito visit for lawn edges, brush, and shaded paths ticks favor. Not sold on its own.

How It Works

  1. Inspection: we walk your property and identify mosquito hotspots — standing water, dense vegetation, shaded damp areas, and entry points from neighbouring properties.
  2. Barrier Spray Treatment: a targeted barrier spray applied to trees, shrubs, fence lines, and ground cover. It bonds to foliage and kills mosquitoes on contact, then keeps working for up to 21 days.
  3. Breeding Source Control: where possible, we advise on eliminating standing water sources — bird baths, low spots, clogged gutters — to reduce future breeding.
  4. Follow-Up Service: for seasonal programs, we return every 21 days throughout mosquito season to maintain your protection barrier.

Most treatments take 20–40 minutes. You can return to your yard once the product has dried — typically within 30 minutes.

Is It Safe for Kids and Pets?

Yes. We use EPA/PMRA (Health Canada Pest Management Regulatory Agency) registered products, applied by trained technicians, with timing that keeps kids, pets, and edible gardens in mind. Once the spray has dried — usually about 30 minutes after application — your yard is safe to use normally again. We don't make safety claims beyond what the product labels actually support; mention any specific concerns (a vegetable garden, a pool, a pet with particular sensitivities) when you book.

Frequently Asked Questions — Residential Mosquito Control

How often do seasonal treatments happen?

Every 21 days through mosquito season (roughly May to September). That interval matches how long a barrier treatment holds and how quickly a new generation of mosquitoes can hatch if conditions are right.

Do I need a season-long program, or is one treatment enough?

A single treatment works for a specific window — an event, a few weeks of relief — but it fades. A seasonal program is what actually keeps a yard usable all summer, since it re-treats before the previous application wears off instead of reacting after mosquitoes are already back.

What's the difference between barrier spray and larvicide treatment?

Barrier spray targets adult mosquitoes on the vegetation and surfaces where they rest. Larvicide treatment targets mosquito larvae directly in standing water that can't reasonably be drained or removed — a pond, a low spot that won't dry out. Many properties need both.

Do you treat tick-prone areas too?

Tick control is available as an add-on bundled into a scheduled mosquito visit — lawn edges, brush, and shaded paths ticks favor. It isn't sold as a standalone service.

Is the treatment safe for my vegetable garden?

We use EPA/PMRA-registered products and time application with edible gardens in mind. If you have a vegetable patch, mention it when you book so your technician can plan around it specifically.

What if mosquitoes come back before my next scheduled visit?

Seasonal programs include a 21-day return-visit guarantee — if activity comes back before your next treatment is due, we come back and re-treat at no charge.
