Commercial & Property

Protect the customers actually sitting on your patio.

Scheduled mosquito control for restaurants, breweries, campgrounds, and managed properties — built around your business hours, not a one-off spray.

TL;DR

Commercial mosquito control is a recurring, scheduled service — not a single treatment — because a patio or outdoor seating area needs to stay usable on every service day, not just once. Treatment is timed around your business hours and dries in about 30 minutes, and we can set up multi-property accounts for property management companies.

Built Around Your Business, Not a Homeowner's Schedule

A restaurant patio or campground common area has a different problem than a backyard: it needs to be usable for customers every day you're open, not just occasionally. That means commercial and patio mosquito control runs on a recurring visit schedule, timed around your service hours, so the treatment stays ahead of mosquito activity instead of playing catch-up after customers start complaining.

Mosquito Man Plus is part of the Bugman Plus family, based in Oshawa. We work directly with restaurants, breweries, campgrounds, and property managers across Durham Region and the GTA to set up a schedule and account structure that fits how the business actually operates.

Who This Is For

  • Restaurants and breweries with patio seating — where mosquitoes directly affect whether customers stay outside.
  • Campgrounds — larger outdoor areas where guests spend extended time, often near the water features that also attract mosquitoes.
  • Property management companies — managing multiple properties that each need consistent, accountable service.
  • Any patio or outdoor-seating business where mosquito activity is a customer-experience problem, not just a nuisance.

How It Works

  1. Site walk-through: we assess seating areas, foot traffic patterns, and any standing water or vegetation on the property that's contributing to mosquito activity.
  2. Scheduled treatment: recurring visits timed to your slower hours where possible, so the patio is ready to reopen well before your next rush.
  3. Ongoing account relationship: a consistent schedule and point of contact, not a one-off booking each time.
  4. Multi-property coordination: for property managers, one account covering several addresses on a shared or staggered schedule.

How Much Does Commercial Mosquito Control Cost?

Pricing depends on property size, seating capacity, how many properties are involved, and how often you need service. We'll work out a schedule and price for your specific property — tell us about the business and we'll give you a real quote before anything's booked.

Is It Safe Around Customers and Staff?

Yes. We use EPA/PMRA (Health Canada Pest Management Regulatory Agency) registered products, and the product dries within about 30 minutes with no meaningful residue afterward. We work with you to schedule around your service hours so treatment happens when the patio isn't in active use, and the space is ready to reopen once it's dry.

Frequently Asked Questions — Commercial Mosquito Control

Can you treat around customers and staff during business hours?

We work with you to schedule treatment outside your busiest hours where possible — early morning or between service periods — so it doesn't interrupt customers on the patio.

Do you offer accounts for multiple properties, like property management companies?

Yes. If you manage several properties, tell us the addresses and typical needs and we'll work out a consistent schedule and quote across the portfolio rather than booking each one separately.

How often do commercial properties typically need treatment?

Most commercial patios and outdoor dining areas are treated on a recurring schedule through the season, similar to a residential seasonal program, since a single visit fades and outdoor seating needs to stay usable every service day, not just once.

Is there any closure time needed after treatment?

The product dries in about 30 minutes. We can time treatment for a slow period so the patio is ready to reopen well before your next rush.

Do you provide invoicing and documentation for business accounts?

Yes — ask about business account setup when you request a quote and we'll set up invoicing that fits how your business handles vendor payments.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Restaurants and breweries with patio seating, campgrounds, and other patio or property-management businesses where customers or guests spend time outdoors.

Ready to set up service for your property?

We serve Oshawa, Whitby, Ajax, Pickering, Clarington, and the rest of Durham Region and the GTA. Tell us about your property and we'll put together a schedule and quote.
