Why Mosquito Myths Persist in Scarborough Backyards

Every summer, homeowners across Scarborough try the same handful of tricks to keep mosquitoes away from patios, decks, and backyard gatherings. Some of these ideas have been passed down for generations, others come from social media, and a few are simply marketing claims from products that don't do much once they're actually tested in a real backyard. Before spending money on gadgets or home remedies, it helps to know what's myth and what's fact when it comes to mosquito control in Scarborough and the surrounding GTA.

Myth: Bug Zappers and Citronella Candles Solve the Problem

Bug zappers are a classic backyard fixture, and citronella candles show up on nearly every patio table in the summer. The truth is that both have a very limited effect on the mosquitoes actually biting you. Zappers tend to attract and kill far more harmless insects than mosquitoes, since mosquitoes aren't strongly drawn to light the way moths are. Citronella can mask your scent slightly in a small radius, but it doesn't reduce the mosquito population nearby or stop them from finding you a few feet away. If you're relying on these tools alone in Scarborough or Whitby, you're likely still getting bitten.

Fact: Standing Water Is the Real Source of the Problem

Mosquitoes need still water to lay eggs and complete their life cycle, and it doesn't take much. Clogged gutters, plant saucers, birdbaths, tarps that collect rainwater, and even bottle caps can become breeding sites. In neighborhoods across Durham Region and Scarborough, one of the most effective things a homeowner can do is walk the property after rain and dump out anything holding water. This single habit, repeated weekly through mosquito season, often has a bigger impact than any spray or candle.

Myth: All Mosquito Treatments Are the Same

There's a common assumption that any mosquito spray, whether from a hardware store or a professional company, works the same way and produces the same results. In reality, application matters as much as the product. Mosquitoes rest in shaded, humid spots during the day, tall grass, dense shrubs, under decks, along fence lines, so treatments need to target those resting areas specifically, not just open lawn space. A professional approach also considers wind, rainfall timing, and reapplication intervals, which is a big part of why do-it-yourself sprays often disappoint homeowners in Ajax and Pickering who've tried them without seeing lasting results.

Fact: Professional Treatments Use Registered, Regulated Products

Mosquito Man Plus, based in Oshawa, uses EPA/PMRA-registered products as part of its treatment approach, which means the products have gone through federal review for safety and effectiveness when applied according to label directions. This isn't the same as spraying an over-the-counter concentrate from a garden hose attachment. Registered products applied correctly to vegetation and resting sites can meaningfully knock down mosquito activity for weeks, which is especially useful heading into long weekends or backyard events in Whitby, Ajax, or Clarington.

Myth: One Treatment Will Keep Your Yard Mosquito-Free All Summer

It would be convenient if a single spray in June solved the problem through September, but that's not how mosquito biology works. New mosquitoes are constantly hatching from nearby water sources, migrating in from neighboring properties, ravines, or wetlands, and repopulating treated areas over time. This is why seasonal programs exist. Mosquito Man Plus offers a return-visit guarantee on seasonal programs, which reflects the reality that ongoing management, not a one-time fix, is what actually keeps mosquito numbers down through a Scarborough or Oshawa summer.

Fact: Location and Landscaping Affect Your Mosquito Pressure

Not every property in the GTA deals with the same level of mosquito activity. Homes backing onto ravines, ponds, or wooded areas, common in parts of Scarborough as well as Whitby and Pickering, tend to have higher mosquito pressure simply because there's more natural habitat nearby. Dense landscaping, mulch beds that stay damp, and low-lying yards that pool water after rain all contribute as well. Understanding your property's specific risk factors is part of building an effective control plan, rather than assuming every yard needs the exact same approach.

Myth: DIY Yard Sprays Are Just as Effective as Professional Service

Store-bought yard sprays can offer some short-term relief, but they typically lack the concentration, targeted application method, and reapplication schedule that professional treatments use. Many homeowners in Ajax find that a DIY spray knocks down mosquitoes for a few days before activity returns to normal. Professional programs are built around repeat visits timed to local mosquito life cycles, which is a more realistic way to manage populations over an entire season rather than chasing short-term dips.

What Actually Works for Mosquito Control in Scarborough and the GTA

Combining a few habits tends to produce the best results. Eliminate standing water weekly, keep grass and shrubs trimmed to reduce resting spots, and consider a professional program if your property backs onto green space or has a history of heavy mosquito activity.
